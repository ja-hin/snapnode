AI email assistant FAQ

Questions teams ask before launching AI email automation

Will this replace my team?

No. The model is designed to improve team productivity, speed, and consistency. People stay in control and can take over whenever needed.

Can it really reply based on our company details?

Yes. It can be trained from your website, uploaded material, brochures, FAQs, and other approved business information.

Can we control when it replies?

Yes. You can define response timing, working-hour logic, delay behaviour, review needs, and escalation paths.

What if a person wants to take over the thread?

That is built into the system. Human-in-the-Loop mode allows a person to edit, continue, or fully take over any conversation from the same workflow.
