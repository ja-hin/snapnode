AI email assistant FAQ

Questions teams ask before launching AI email automation in Gmail, shared inboxes, sales, and support workflows

Will this replace my team or work alongside them?

No. The model is designed to improve team productivity, speed, consistency, and coverage. People stay in control and can review, edit, or take over whenever needed.

Can it really reply based on our company details and past email context?

Yes. It can be trained from your website, uploaded material, brochures, FAQs, approved documents, and the thread context relevant to the reply.

Can we control when it replies and when a human approves first?

Yes. You can define response timing, working-hour logic, delay behaviour, review needs, and escalation paths.

What if a person wants to take over the thread?

That is built into the system. Human-in-the-Loop mode allows a person to edit, continue, or fully take over any conversation from the same workflow.
